A wealthy business owner hosted a party at their cottage.
During the party, a locker hidden behind a bookshelf in
the Study Room was robbed. Eight people were present in the
cottage and one of them is the culprit. You are the
detective hired by the owner's PA to solve the case.

Good luck, Detective.
